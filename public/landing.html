Questions

The things people ask first

Do my staff need to install anything?

No. That's the whole point. You add your team once — each person gets a short name like @ravi or @sunita — and from then on they get jobs as normal WhatsApp messages from your number — the one already saved in their phone — and reply with a single character. There's nothing to download, no account, no password, and nothing to teach anyone.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. Parchi connects to the number you already use, the same way WhatsApp Web does on a laptop. You scan a code once, or type an 8-digit code if you only have your phone. Your chats, your groups and your contacts all stay exactly as they are.

Can I still use WhatsApp normally?

Yes. Nothing changes about how you use it. Parchi just watches for the moment you mention one of your team. Everything else carries on as before — you'll see Parchi listed under Linked Devices, and you can remove it any time.

Is my number safe? I've heard of people getting blocked.

You've heard right, and it's worth being straight about. Numbers get blocked for sending bulk or marketing messages to people who didn't ask. Parchi never does that — there is no broadcast feature, and we only reply to people who messaged you first, plus your own staff. That's the lowest-risk way to use WhatsApp there is. We'll explain exactly how the connection works before you set anything up.

Can you read my customer conversations?

We only keep messages that become jobs, plus a seven-day window so you can still assign something from yesterday. Everything else is discarded, never stored. Your old chats are never uploaded anywhere.

What if my work comes in a group, not a direct chat?

That works too — mention the person on the message in the group. Worth mentioning because most tools built on WhatsApp's official business API can't see group chats at all.

When can I actually use it?

It's working today and being used by a real business. We're adding a small number of early users so we can set each one up properly rather than all at once. Leave your details and we'll come to you.
